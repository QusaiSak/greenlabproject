Write an analytical essay explaining how the company can approach this problem through four connected areas of Green IT: energy-efficient data centers, carbon-aware software, electronic waste (e-waste), and the overall ICT carbon footprint.

For carbon-aware software, explain how software engineers can influence environmental impact through efficient algorithms, reducing unnecessary computation and data movement, selecting appropriate models, optimizing AI inference, and scheduling workloads. Explain how electricity carbon intensity can vary depending on location and time, and how workloads could potentially be shifted toward lower-carbon periods or regions. Discuss constraints such as latency, deadlines, availability, network usage, and data-location requirements.
